# SparkIQ requirements

## Product

SparkIQ must accept any rough idea and produce a launch-ready strategy through ten separate AI stages. Each stage returns structured JSON, can be approved or edited, and can be regenerated without discarding the rest of the brand context.

## Safety and quality

- API keys exist only in the backend.
- Provider failures have visible loading, error, and retry states.
- Competitor suggestions are explicitly marked for verification.
- Cliche checks, written score reasons, consistency scoring, and targeted autofix data are part of the model contract.
- The final kit displays strategy and guidelines rather than prompts, infrastructure, or raw JSON.

## User experience

The interface is mobile-friendly, metallic blue, stage-based, and includes a floating founder chat. It supports PDF/share output, a poster preview, language selection, launch content, e-commerce advice, feedback demo, and voice-over control.
